Tu tarea: Recomendación formal al comité del hogar

You and your partner have finished your household negotiation. Now write a formal recommendation to a Comité de Bienestar del Hogar (Household Welfare Committee) proposing a fair weekly chore schedule based on your negotiation.

Your recommendation must include:

  • A formal opening phrase (see model below)
  • At least three reasons for your proposed distribution — drawn directly from what you and your partner said during the negotiation (use your Paso 3 table and Paso 5 synthesis sentence)
  • At least two formal adverb expressions from the Register-Shift Table above
  • At least one reflexive verb in present tense (e.g., se levanta, se encarga)
  • A formal closing phrase
Model opening: "Estimado Comité de Bienestar del Hogar: Por medio de la presente, les comunicamos la propuesta de distribución de quehaceres acordada entre los miembros de nuestra familia, con el objetivo de garantizar una convivencia justa y equitativa."

Model closing: "Agradecemos su consideración y quedamos a disposición para cualquier consulta adicional. Atentamente, [nombre del estudiante]"
Lista de verificación — Self-Correction Checklist
  • I used at least three reflexive verbs in present tense correctly (e.g., me levanto, se encarga, se ducha). Check each reflexive verb: does it have the correct pronoun (me/te/se) AND the correct -ar/-er/-ir ending?
  • I used gustar + infinitive to express at least two preferences or complaints (e.g., no me gusta limpiar el baño; le gusta cocinar la cena). Each gustar sentence needs: indirect object pronoun (me/le) + gusta + infinitive verb.
  • I referenced the specific chore assignments negotiated in Paso 3 — naming who is responsible for what and why. Look at your Paso 3 table. Did you name at least three chores and who is responsible for each?
  • I included the Paso 5 synthesis sentence: "En nuestra casa ideal, [persona] se encarga de [quehacer] porque..." Copy or adapt your Paso 5 sentence — this is the core of your recommendation.
  • I used at least two formal adverb replacements from the Register-Shift Table (e.g., de manera constante instead of siempre; en ocasiones instead of a veces). Underline each formal adverb you used and draw an arrow to the informal version it replaces.
