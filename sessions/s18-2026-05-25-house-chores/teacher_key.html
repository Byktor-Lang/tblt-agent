DO NOT DISTRIBUTE — TEACHER KEY — House Chores Negotiation
Activity Overview
Session: s18-2026-05-25-house-chores  |  Course: Spanish General (9th Grade)  |  Class: spanish_general_9th
Topic: House chores and household responsibilities  |  Gap type: Resource gap  |  Paso structure: categorization_interview_synthesis  |  Complication: role_asymmetry
Grammar 1: Present tense of reflexive verbs (levantarse, ducharse, lavarse, vestirse, acostarse)
Grammar 2: gustar + infinitive (me gusta / no me gusta / le gusta)
PVS (12 items): barrer el piso, lavar los platos, limpiar el baño, pasar la aspiradora, sacar la basura, hacer la cama, ordenar el cuarto, cocinar la cena, levantarse temprano, lavarse las manos, ducharse, la tarea doméstica
Transfer goal: Write a formal recommendation to a household committee proposing a fair weekly chore schedule with at least three reasons drawn from the negotiation.
Role Cards — What Each Student Holds (Private Information)
Student A (El/La padre/madre): Does 6 chores (barrer, lavar platos, limpiar baño, pasar aspiradora, sacar basura, cocinar cena). Schedule: levantarse 5:30, ducharse. Dislikes: limpiar el baño, barrer el piso alone. Goal: redistribute 3+ chores.
Student B (El/La hijo/a): Does 2 things (ordenar cuarto sometimes, lavarse manos). Schedule: levantarse 7:00, ducharse. Likes: cocinar sometimes. Dislikes: barrer el piso. Constraint: sports practice 3 days/week. Goal: accept some chores, protect after-school time.
Gap structure: Student A has a full chore load but no knowledge of B's schedule constraints or cooking preference. Student B has a light load but real schedule conflicts. Resolution requires A to probe B's actual preferences using gustar and B to counter-propose specific chores. Neither can complete the negotiation without the other's information.
Facilitator Notes — Paso by Paso
Paso 1 (2–3 min): Individual classification. Expected sort: Household tasks = barrer el piso, lavar los platos, limpiar el baño, hacer la cama. Personal routines = ducharse, lavarse las manos. Students may argue for "hacer la cama" as personal routine — accept with justification (the classification debate is pedagogically valuable; it primes vocabulary for Paso 3).
Paso 2 (3–4 min): Sentence production. Monitor that students use a reflexive verb AND gustar. Common errors: forgetting the reflexive pronoun ("levanto" instead of "me levanto"), using "gusta" without the indirect object pronoun. Circulate and prompt: "¿Cómo se dice 'I get up'?"
Paso 3 (10–12 min, core): The negotiation. Enforce the role-play: A must propose redistribution of at least 3 chores; B must accept some and counter-propose on others. If students default to English or simply agree immediately, prompt: "¿Le gusta a tu pareja limpiar el baño? ¿Por qué o por qué no?" Minimum requirement: 3 negotiated assignments recorded in the table.
Paso 4 (3–4 min): Fairness rating. If partners disagree on the rating, push them to negotiate a consensus — this is a second negotiation layer that builds on Paso 3. Require at least one gustar sentence and one reflexive verb in the written explanation.
Paso 5 (5–7 min): Class synthesis. Each pair reads their synthesis sentences. Class votes on "la distribución más justa." After the vote, each pair commits publicly. Record the most common chore assignments on the board for transfer-goal scaffolding.
Success Criteria Checkpoints
SC 1: Use at least three reflexive verbs in present tense correctly. Watch for: levantarse, ducharse, lavarse. Penalize missing reflexive pronoun (levanto ≠ me levanto).
SC 2: Use gustar + infinitive for at least two preferences. Watch for: "me gusta cocinar," "no me gusta barrer." Penalize bare infinitive without gustar ("quiero cocinar" satisfies a different criterion).
SC 3: Negotiate a specific chore assignment for each person. Minimum: 3 rows in Paso 3 table, each with an assigned person and a stated reason.
SC 4: Produce the Paso 5 synthesis: "En nuestra casa ideal, [persona] se encarga de [quehacer] porque..." — must cite a gustar or reflexive-verb reason.
SC 5: Write the formal recommendation (post-task) using formal opening phrases and frequency-to-formal-adverb register shift. Assessed in post-task artifact, not here.
Dialect and Language Notes
Target variety: Latin American Spanish. Excluded features: vosotros, vos, leísmo. If students produce "vosotros," redirect to "ustedes." If students write "lavarse" with a vos conjugation, note but do not penalize — address in feedback.
Preferred lexemes: "la computadora" (not "el ordenador"), "el autobús" (not "el colectivo"), "el apartamento" (not "el piso"). The topic is domestic, so these lexeme preferences are unlikely to surface; monitor if students stray into general vocabulary.

Transfer Goal Preview (Pre-Task Connection)
The post-task (Round 3) asks students to write a formal recommendation to a household committee. The main task negotiation provides the content: chore assignments negotiated in Paso 3, fairness ratings from Paso 4, and synthesis sentences from Paso 5. Students should be told: "Save your Paso 3 table and Paso 5 sentences — you will need them for the writing task."
Register bridge: The spoken negotiation is informal. The writing task requires formal register. Students will practice the shift in the post-task via the Register-Shift Table (frequency-to-formal-adverb pattern: a veces → en ocasiones; siempre → de manera constante; nunca → en ningún caso).
