DO NOT DISTRIBUTE — POST-TASK TEACHER COPY — Formal Recommendation
Scenario Anchor — Design Parameters
Genre: formal_recommendation — A formal recommendation memo or letter addressed to an institution (Comité de Bienestar del Hogar), arguing for a specific course of action (fair chore redistribution) with supporting reasons.
Audience: Comité de Bienestar del Hogar (Household Welfare Committee) — a fictional institutional body; formal register required throughout.
Complication: role_asymmetry — The underlying negotiation was between a parent (full chore load) and a teenager (very few chores), with structural power imbalance shaping the discussion. The recommendation must propose a redistribution that acknowledges both parties' constraints and preferences.
Outcome: A negotiated chore distribution with at least 3 assigned chores (one per person minimum), a fairness rating justification, and a Paso 5 public commitment synthesis sentence — all of which feed directly into the recommendation's content.
Stakes line: Students are arguing to an institutional body that their negotiated plan is the fairest possible outcome. The recommendation must be persuasive and formally composed — informal register or unsupported claims weaken the case.
Register-shift pattern: frequency_to_formal_adverb — informal frequency expressions (a veces, siempre, nunca, todos los días) replaced by formal adverbials (en ocasiones, de manera constante, en ningún caso, diariamente).
Transfer goal: Write a formal recommendation to a household committee proposing a fair weekly chore schedule, with at least three reasons drawn from the negotiation.
ADB Coherence Audit — Post-Task Coverage
activity_pvs_items_used (12 items): Register-shift table examples embed barrer el piso, sacar la basura, limpiar el baño, and tareas domésticas. Writing prompt requires naming chores from Paso 3 table — all 12 PVS items are available for student use. Checklist item 3 explicitly requires Paso 3 chore names.
confirmed_complication (role_asymmetry): Writing prompt requires three reasons drawn from the negotiation — reasons must reference both parties' constraints (parent's full schedule, teenager's preferences). Model opening and scenario anchor both embed the family context. Students who address the asymmetry explicitly write stronger recommendations.
confirmed_outcome (Paso 3 assignments + Paso 5 synthesis): Checklist item 3 requires Paso 3 table references; checklist item 4 requires the Paso 5 synthesis sentence verbatim. These two checklist items are the direct link between main task output and post-task writing.
gap_summary (A holds full schedule, B holds preferences): The formal recommendation's persuasive structure requires students to demonstrate they understood both sides of the information gap: naming what A currently does (full schedule) and what B offered or agreed to (preferences + constraints). Students who only write from one perspective will fail checklist item 3.
Register-Shift Assessment Notes
Target pattern: frequency_to_formal_adverb — informal frequency adverbs replaced by formal equivalents.
Pairs assessed:
Informal (accept in spoken Pasos) Formal required in recommendation Check for
a veces en ocasiones Adverb phrase at sentence boundary, not mid-clause informal placement
siempre de manera constante Full phrase (not just "constantemente" — the table specifies the formal phrase)
nunca en ningún caso Sentence-initial placement typical of formal Spanish; check word order
todos los días diariamente Single-word adverb — most accessible of the four; a student who uses "diariamente" correctly shows solid register awareness
Minimum requirement: 2 formal adverb replacements (checklist item 5). Full credit for 3+. A student using only "de manera constante" once does not meet the minimum.
Facilitation Notes — Post-Task Sequencing
Before distributing: Tell students to take out their Paso 3 negotiation table and Paso 5 synthesis sentence from the main task. These are the primary source materials for the recommendation.
Register shift warm-up (2 min): Ask 2–3 students: "How do you say 'a veces' in formal Spanish?" Then "siempre?" — confirm from the table before students begin writing. This 2-minute investment prevents the most common register error.
Writing time (12–15 min): Individual. Students use the model opening and closing as frames. Monitor that students are referencing their Paso 3 table (not inventing new chore assignments). Circulate and prompt on checklist items 3 and 4 if students seem to be writing generically rather than from their negotiation.
Self-correction (3 min): Students use the checklist to self-correct before handing in. Checklist item 5 (underline formal adverbs + draw arrow to informal version) is a useful self-assessment move — students who cannot draw the arrow did not make the register shift consciously.
Time budget: Total post-task = 17–20 min. With main task (25–30 min) + pre-task (15–18 min) the full lesson is ~60–68 min — slightly over the 47-min block. Plan to assign the recommendation as guided homework if time runs short, using the Paso 3 table taken home.
