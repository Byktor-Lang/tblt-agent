Facilitation Notes — Exercise by Exercise
Exercise 1 (RANK, 5–6 min): Individual. No "correct" ranking — the goal is vocabulary activation and personal association. Monitor that students write rankings as numbers 1–8. The two reporting sentences are required; monitor use of "porque" + a reason. Quick class share after: ask 2–3 students their top-effort chore to preview disagreements that will emerge in the main task.
Exercise 2 (ODD, 4–5 min): Individual. The key requirement is a semantic reason — not "it sounds different" or "I don't know this word." Group A: odd one out = lavar los platos (household task, not a personal hygiene routine; the others involve personal body care or waking up). Group B: odd one out = cocinar la cena (food preparation, not a cleaning task; the others involve cleaning floors/surfaces/rooms). Group C: odd one out = ducharse (personal hygiene routine, not a household chore; the others involve maintaining the household/shared spaces). Accept alternative justified answers — the reasoning quality matters more than the specific choice.
Exercise 3 (WB, 6–7 min): Individual. Word bank has 12 items; sentences need 10. The two distractors are hacer la cama and me visto. Monitor that students do not leave sentence 5 (double-blank) incomplete — it requires both "me encargo de" and a chore choice. This sentence previews the main-task Paso 5 synthesis frame most directly.
