Los quehaceres en casa — House Chores Negotiation
9th Grade Spanish · Resource Gap Activity · Student A
Your Role Card — El/La padre/madre (READ THIS FIRST — DO NOT SHOW YOUR PARTNER)

Who you are: You are the parent in the household. You work full-time and currently do most of the chores. You are exhausted and want a fairer arrangement. You have real power in this negotiation — your teenager depends on you — but you also want peace at home, so you cannot simply demand everything.

Your current chore schedule:

  • Barrer el piso — every day
  • Lavar los platos — every day after dinner
  • Limpiar el baño — three times a week
  • Pasar la aspiradora — twice a week
  • Sacar la basura — every other day
  • Cocinar la cena — every night

Your daily routine (use this in Paso 2 and Paso 3):
Me levanto a las 5:30. Me ducho rápido. Me visto y salgo al trabajo.

Your preferences (use gustar in Paso 3):
No me gusta limpiar el baño todos los días. No me gusta barrer el piso solo/a.

Your negotiation goal: Redistribute at least three chores to your teenager. You must use gustar to express what you dislike and reflexive verbs to describe your exhausting routine. Stay in your role.

CONFIDENTIAL — Do not show this card to your partner. Only share information when your partner asks a direct question in Spanish.
Vocabulary bank (Banco de palabras): barrer el piso · lavar los platos · limpiar el baño · pasar la aspiradora · sacar la basura · hacer la cama · ordenar el cuarto · cocinar la cena · levantarse temprano · lavarse las manos · ducharse · la tarea doméstica
Paso 1 — Clasifica los quehaceres (Classify the chores)

Look at the six chores below. Sort each one into the correct column: Quehaceres del hogar (household tasks) or Rutinas personales (personal routines). Work individually — you will compare with your partner in Paso 3.

Quehaceres del hogar (Household tasks) Rutinas personales (Personal routines)

Quehaceres a clasificar: barrer el piso · lavar los platos · limpiar el baño · hacer la cama · ducharse · lavarse las manos

Sentence frame for reporting in Paso 5:

"[Quehacer] es una tarea doméstica / una rutina personal."
Paso 2 — Mis quehaceres en casa (My chores at home)

From your role card, choose two chores you do regularly. Write one sentence about each using a reflexive verb and one sentence using gustar. You will use these sentences in Paso 3.

"Yo me [reflexive verb] a las [time] para [quehacer]."
Example: "Me levanto a las 5:30 para cocinar la cena."
"No me gusta [quehacer] porque [reason]."
Example: "No me gusta limpiar el baño porque tarda mucho tiempo."
Paso 3 — La negociación en casa (The household negotiation)

Now speak with your partner. You are the parent; your partner is the teenager. Use your role card information and the sentences from Paso 2. Ask your partner about their routine and preferences. Then negotiate a fairer chore distribution.

Model dialogue (continue from here):

Student A (padre/madre) Student B (hijo/a)
"Yo me levanto a las 5:30 y barro el piso todos los días. ¿A qué hora te levantas tú?" (Answer with your real schedule from your role card)
"No me gusta lavar los platos todos los días. ¿Te gusta lavar los platos?" (Respond honestly — you must give a real answer, not just "sí" or "no" — explain)
(Propose a redistribution: "Yo propongo que tú te encargues de [chore] porque…") (Agree or counter-propose with a reason using gustar or a reflexive verb)

Record the negotiated assignments below (you need at least 3):

Chore (Quehacer) Assigned to (Se encarga) Reason given (Razón)
Paso 4 — ¿Es justo? (Is it fair?)

Look at the chore assignments you recorded in Paso 3. Rate how fair the new distribution is on a scale of 1 (not fair at all) to 5 (completely fair). Then explain your rating using gustar and at least one reflexive verb.

Fairness rating: 12345
"Esta distribución [es / no es] justa porque [gustar reason]. Además, yo me [reflexive verb] [time/how] y por eso [reason for assignment]."

If your partner disagrees with your rating, note their reason and try to reach a consensus score. Write the consensus score: ____

Paso 5 — La casa ideal (The ideal household) — Class Synthesis

Using the chore assignments from Paso 3 and the fairness rating from Paso 4, complete the synthesis sentence below for each chore you negotiated. Your pair will share this with the class. The class will vote on the fairest plan and each pair makes a public commitment.

"En nuestra casa ideal, [persona] se encarga de [quehacer] porque [razón usando gustar o verbo reflexivo]."

Public commitment: After the class vote, your pair states one chore assignment you both commit to. Circle the chore and write your commitment:

"Nosotros nos comprometemos a que [persona] se encargue de [quehacer] a partir de ahora."
