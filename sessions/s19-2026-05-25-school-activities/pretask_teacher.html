Pre-Task Overview
Exercise sequence: ASSOC → MC → FREQ | PVS subset used: 10/12 items (all 6 subject names + interesante + aburrido/a + difícil + fácil; el recreo and el proyecto escolar in FREQ survey) | Target structures: gustar + noun (me gusta, te gusta, le gusta, nos gusta), ser + adjective agreement | Timing: 3 + 6 + 7 = 16 min
Exercise 1 — ASSOC Notes
Purpose and facilitation
ASSOC activates prior schema before vocabulary exposure — do not pre-teach any words before this exercise. Accept English words and cognates (math, history). After Exercise 3, ask students to call out their Bubble 1 and Bubble 2 words; write them on the board and circle any that appeared in Exercises 2–3. This connects prior knowledge to the PVS explicitly.
Exercise 2 — MC Answer Key
Facilitation note
Read the paragraph aloud once at natural pace before students choose options. Remind students that gustar agrees with the following noun (subject of the sentence), not with the person who likes it. Monitor for Blank 5 — the most common error is selecting (b) le gustan (wrong person) instead of (c) les gustan.

Exercise 3 — FREQ Facilitation Notes
Interview facilitation
Circulate during the FREQ survey. Ensure students are asking the question aloud ("¿Te gusta la historia?") — not silently filling in squares. The survey primes the preference vocabulary that will appear in Main Task Paso 2 and Paso 3. The reporting sentences require both le gusta (third-person indirect object) and porque es — two target structures in one sentence. Accept le gusta mucho / un poco / no le gusta as valid expansions of the frame.
Expected reporting sentences (sample)
A mi compañero/a le gusta la música porque es interesante.
A mi compañero/a no le gusta la historia porque es aburrida y es difícil.
A mi compañero/a le gusta la educación física porque es fácil y divertida.
Answer Key Summary
MC Paragraph — Full corrected version
En mi colegio hay muchas materias diferentes. A mí me gusta la educación física porque es divertida. La clase de matemáticas es muy difícil, pero a muchos estudiantes les gustan. El recreo no es una materia, pero nos gusta a todos porque es fácil y divertido. La historia y la música son muy interesantes, pero la tarea es aburrida.
