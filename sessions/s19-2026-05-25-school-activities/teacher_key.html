DO NOT DISTRIBUTE — TEACHER KEY
¿Cuál es la mejor clase? — School Subject Ranking Debate — Teacher Key
Activity Design Overview
Type: Opinion Ranking Debate (Oral Gap) | Paso structure: opinion_ranking_debate | Complication: resource_scarcity (#1 spot scarcity) | Grammar 1: gustar + noun/infinitive | Grammar 2: ser + adjective (school contexts) | PVS: 12 items | Timing: 5+6+8+7+6 = 32 min core (15 min margin in 47-min block)
Paso-by-Paso Teacher Notes
Paso 1 Notes — Individual ranking (5 min)
Students work silently. Circulate to check that sentences use me gusta (not gustar alone) and that the adjective follows es (not está). Common error: *Me gusta porque difícil — prompt: "¿Es difícil o no es difícil?". Ensure no pair-consultation yet.
Paso 2 Notes — Pair share (6 min)
Students share aloud (not read). Monitor for use of both gustar AND ser in the same sentence. If only one structure is heard, prompt: "¿Y cómo es esa clase?" The partner record section ensures active listening. Allow clarification questions in English but target-language responses only.
Paso 3 Notes — Debate (8 min)
This is the information-gap core. Students will likely disagree on rank 1 and rank 6 most. The model dialogue shows a two-exchange minimum — redirect students who agree immediately without arguing: "¿Estás seguro/a? ¿No hay ninguna diferencia entre ustedes?" The joint top-3 negotiation is the bridge to Paso 4.
Paso 4 Notes — Complication (7 min)
Resource scarcity complication: Only one subject can hold #1. Both students must advocate for their own Paso 1 choice and negotiate toward a consensus. Students who capitulate immediately (no argument) should be prompted: "¿Por qué crees que tu materia es mejor? Da dos razones con 'gustar' y 'ser'." The written justification (2 reasons) is the language-production target for this Paso — collect these for formative assessment.
Paso 5 Notes — Class declaration (6 min)
Each pair reports: (1) their individual Paso 1 #1s, (2) their Paso 4 agreed #1. Tally on the board (bar or tally marks). The class declaration sentence frame must be completed by every pair using their own Paso 4 agreed subject — not the class winner. The reconsideration sentence (for pairs whose subject did not win) is a low-stakes production boost — accept any grammatically adequate response. Ending type: tally/profile (visual artifact builds during Paso 5).
