Planes para el fin de semana — Teacher Key

Session s17-2026-05-25-weekend-plans | Round 1 | Oral Gap

Activity Overview

Gap type: Oral information gap. Student A and Student B hold mirror-inverted schedules. A wants el cine; B wants el parque.

Complication: values_conflict — preferences differ; only one shared free time slot exists. Students must use poder + infinitivo and tener que + infinitivo to negotiate a mutually acceptable plan.

Target grammar: (1) poder + infinitivo, (2) tener que + infinitivo.

Task resolution: The only mutually free slot is Saturday afternoon (A free) — but B is busy Sat afternoon. The only slot where both are free is the sábado por la mañana (B free, A busy) OR any slot found through careful comparison. Teacher note: there is deliberately no slot where both are free at the same time — students must compromise on either the activity OR accept that one person gives up their preference. This productive tension drives negotiation.

Timing: Paso 1 (~5 min), Paso 2 (~7 min), Paso 3 (~10 min), Paso 4 (~5 min), Paso 5 (~7 min) = ~34 min core. Leave 13 min for setup, transitions, and closing with the 47-min block.

Paso-by-Paso Teacher Notes

Paso 1: Circulate and check that students are writing tener que + infinitivo correctly (not "tengo que" + noun). Remind students this is private — don't show partner.

Paso 2: Model the interview with a student before pairs begin. Confirm students are using question forms: ¿Cuándo estás libre? ¿Por qué no puedes?

Paso 3: This is the productive conflict zone. Expect frustration when students discover no shared free slot. Coach: "¿Cuál es más importante para ti, la actividad o el tiempo?" Validate creative compromises.

Paso 4: Quick evaluation — 1–2 minutes per pair. Use this to gauge student buy-in before public announcement.

Paso 5: Pairs read aloud. Jot the activity/time on the board to build a class "weekend plan map." Ask: "¿Quién tiene el plan más interesante?"

Success Criteria Observation Guide

1. tener que + infinitivo constraint: Listen for "Tengo que [verb]..." — not "tengo [noun]".

2. poder + infinitivo availability: Listen for "Puedo / No puedo + [infinitivo]" in Paso 2 and Paso 3.

3. Negotiation in Spanish: Pairs staying in Spanish throughout Paso 3 = criterion met.

4. Paso 5 plan sentence: Sentence must contain day + time of day + activity + porque + reason.

5. Post-task email: Assessed separately at the post-task stage.

Answer Key — Possible Paso 5 Plan Sentences

Sample 1 (compromise on activity, not time): "El sábado por la tarde vamos al parque porque los dos podemos y es un buen plan."

(Note: Only A is free Sat afternoon — this is only possible if B concedes their constraint, which is a valid but non-default compromise.)

Sample 2 (creative resolution): "El domingo por la tarde vamos al cine porque Estudiante B está libre y Estudiante A puede cambiar el partido."

Sample 3 (both give up something): "El sábado por la mañana vamos al parque porque Estudiante B está libre y Estudiante A puede estudiar más tarde."

Any logically consistent plan sentence with tener que and poder forms is acceptable. Assess on grammatical form, not on which activity they chose.
