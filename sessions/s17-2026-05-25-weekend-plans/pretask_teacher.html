Pre-Task Purpose and ADB Alignment

This pre-task primes the 10 PVS items and both target grammar structures (poder + infinitivo, tener que + infinitivo) before students encounter the oral gap Main Task. Exercise sequence: SORT → ODD → FRAME.

ADB alignment: (1) All 10 PVS items activated across three exercises. (2) values_conflict complication primed — schedule logic tested in SORT (students reason about libre/ocupado constraints). (3) Paso 5 plan sentence structure rehearsed in FRAME exercise 4. (4) Both poder and tener que structures produced in FRAME exercises 1–3.

Suggested timing: Exercise 1 (~7 min), Exercise 2 (~7 min), Exercise 3 (~10 min) = ~24 min. Leaves time for transition into Main Task.

Exercise 1 — SORT Answer Key

Logical statements: 1, 2, 4, 6

Illogical statements:

3. Illogical: "Estoy libre... porque tengo que ir al partido" — tener que signals an obligation (being busy), not freedom. The conjunction porque inverts the logical relationship.
5. Illogical: "El lunes por la tarde estoy ocupado/a, así que puedo salir el sábado" — Monday's status has no logical implication for Saturday's availability. The así que (therefore) connector creates a false causal chain across non-adjacent days.

Note: Accept any semantically valid explanation. The key criterion is that students articulate the logical flaw, not that they match this wording exactly.

Exercise 2 — ODD Answer Key

Set A: el domingo — the other three are activities/places; el domingo is a time expression. (Accept: el domingo is a day, not a leisure activity.)
Set B: libre — the other three are time expressions (specific day or span); libre is a schedule status adjective. (Accept any principled semantic distinction.)
Set C: el sábado — the other three are either time-of-day expressions (por la mañana, por la tarde) or a schedule status (ocupado/a); el sábado is a day-of-week label. (Accept: el sábado names a specific day; the others describe parts of the day or availability status.)

Exercise 3 — FRAME Answer Key and Guidance

Frames are open-ended (personally meaningful). Accept any sentence that:

1. Uses tener que + infinitivo correctly with a time expression and an occupation reason.
2. Uses poder + infinitivo correctly with a time expression and a libre status.
3. Uses both structures in sequence — no puedo... porque tengo que... pero puedo...
4. Produces a grammatically correct plan sentence with day + time + activity + porque + reason.

Sample correct answers for frame 4: "El sábado por la tarde vamos al cine porque estamos libres." / "El domingo por la mañana vamos al parque porque no tenemos que hacer nada."

Error to watch for: Students writing "tengo que el partido" (noun instead of infinitive). Redirect: "¿Tienes que IR al partido? Entonces escribe: tengo que ir al partido."
