Part 2 — Formal Email to Parent or Guardian

Task: Write a formal email in Spanish to a parent or guardian confirming your weekend plans.

Your email must:
1. Use a formal salutation (Estimado/a + [title/name])
2. Explain when you had a schedule constraint and why (tener que + infinitivo)
3. Confirm when you are available (poder + infinitivo)
4. State the agreed activity, day, and time from your Paso 5 plan sentence
5. Use a formal closing (Atentamente or Con respeto)

Use your register-shift table from Part 1 to help convert your spoken phrases into formal written language.

Self-Correction Checklist — Review your email before submitting

  • I included a formal salutation (Estimado/a...).
  • I used tener que + infinitivo to explain at least one schedule constraint.
  • I used poder + infinitivo to confirm when I am available.
  • I named the agreed activity, day, and time from our Paso 5 plan.
  • I used a formal closing (Atentamente or Con respeto) and signed my name.
