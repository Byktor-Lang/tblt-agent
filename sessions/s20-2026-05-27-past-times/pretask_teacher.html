Exercise 1 — ASSOC: Teacher Notes

Students brainstorm any Spanish vocabulary related to "los pasatiempos y el tiempo libre" before any input. This is a schema-activation exercise — there are no wrong answers.

Purpose: Students activate prior knowledge before encountering target vocabulary. After Exercise 3, students revisit Exercise 1 and circle any words they predicted correctly — this meta-awareness moment is pedagogically valuable and takes 30 seconds.
Expected brainstorm items (accept any reasonable Spanish):
Sports: fútbol, baloncesto, béisbol, tenis, natación, correr, nadar
Activities: bailar, leer, dibujar, cocinar, escuchar música, ver televisión
Adjectives: divertido, aburrido, emocionante, fácil, difícil
Other: tiempo libre, fin de semana, después de la escuela
Exercise 2 — MC: Answer Key
Blank Context Correct Answer Grammar Point Tested
1 a mí me ___ el fútbol (a) gusta Gustar + singular subject (el fútbol = singular) → gusta, not gustan
2 porque ___ emocionante y activo (a) es Ser + singular subject (el fútbol) → es, not son
3 a nosotros nos ___ la natación y el senderismo (b) gustan Gustar + compound plural subject (la natación y el senderismo = plural) → gustan, not gusta
4 son muy ___ (b) emocionantes Adjective agrees with plural noun phrase la natación y el senderismo → emocionantes (plural); emocionante is singular; emocionado is a past participle (wrong form)
5 El dibujo y bailar ___ (b) son Compound subject (el dibujo y bailar = two items) → plural son
6 pasatiempos muy ___ (b) creativos Adjective modifies plural masculine noun pasatiempos → creativos; creativo is singular; creativa is feminine singular
7 les gusta ___ (b) leer Gustar takes an infinitive as subject → leer (infinitive); lee is a conjugated form; leyendo is a gerund (used differently)
8 Cocinar ___ divertido y ___ (a) es Ser + infinitive subject (cocinar = singular) → es; infinitives function as singular masculine nouns in Spanish
9 divertido y ___ (a) relajante Adjective modifying infinitive subject cocinar → singular; relajante (invariable ending -ante applies to both genders in singular); relajantes is plural; relajado is a past participle
Key teachable moments in this cloze:
  • Blanks 1 vs. 3: contrast between gusta (one subject) and gustan (two subjects) — put these on the board side-by-side after checking answers.
  • Blanks 4 and 6: adjective number agreement — emocionantes and creativos both require plural because they describe plural nouns.
  • Blank 7: gustar + infinitive is a key pattern students will need in the Main Task; reinforce that the infinitive does not change form.
Exercise 3 — FREQ: Facilitation Notes

Students interview their partner using the target grammar. The survey grid collects preference data (me gusta mucho/poco/no me gusta) AND descriptive judgments (adjectives), requiring both gustar and ser + adjective in the same exchange.

Suggested interview exchange script (model for students before they begin):
A: "¿Te gusta el fútbol?"
B: "Sí, me gusta mucho el fútbol. Es emocionante y activo."
A: (marks ✓ under "me gusta mucho," ✓ under "es emocionante," ✓ under "es activo/a")
A: "¿Y la natación?"
Model reporting sentences (accept any grammatically correct sentences):
"A mi compañero/a le gusta el fútbol porque es emocionante y activo."
"A mi compañero/a no le gusta leer porque es aburrido."
"A mi compañero/a le gusta bailar porque es creativo y divertido."
Bridge to Main Task: After Exercise 3, briefly note: "In the next activity, you'll use your own hobby opinions to negotiate with your partner — just like you practiced in the survey. Keep these adjectives in mind!"
