Paso 3 — Compara las listas (4 min) · ORAL — still do not show your list

Take turns sharing your complete ranking verbally. Use: "Mi número [1–5] es [pasatiempo] porque es [adjetivo]." After you hear your partner's full list, note agreements and differences below.

Hobbies we BOTH have in our top 3:

Hobbies where we DISAGREE (I ranked high / partner ranked low, or vice versa):

Paso 4 — El problema: ¡solo hay un lugar! (5 min) · NEGOTIATE
🚨 Complication — Read this together:

Your school can add only ONE new after-school hobby program this year. You and your partner must agree on ONE hobby to recommend. The challenge: you each care about different things in a hobby — one of you prefers hobbies that are creativos y expresivos, and the other prefers hobbies that are activos y emocionantes. You must negotiate and find a hobby you can both defend with gustar and ser.

Negotiate using these frames:

Pienso que [pasatiempo] es mejor porque es más [adjetivo] que [otro pasatiempo].
Pero para mí, [pasatiempo] es importante porque es [adjetivo] y [adjetivo].
Podemos recomendar [pasatiempo] porque nos gusta y es [adjetivo] y [adjetivo].

Our agreed hobby:

Our shared justification (two reasons using gustar and ser):

Be ready to share your sentence with the class — your teacher will call on pairs to compare recommendations!
