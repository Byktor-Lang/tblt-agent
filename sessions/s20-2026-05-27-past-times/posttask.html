Step 1 — Del habla a la escritura formal: Register-Shift Practice (5 min)

In the Main Task you used informal spoken Spanish to negotiate. Now you will write formally. Study how spoken verb phrases change in formal written Spanish, then complete the empty cells.

Informal spoken verb phrase Formal written equivalent Change

Step 2 — Escribe tu recomendación formal (15 min)

Write a formal recommendation letter to the school activities coordinator (la coordinadora de actividades estudiantiles) proposing the hobby your pair agreed on in the Main Task. Use the hobby and the two reasons your pair agreed on — now rewritten in formal Spanish.

Your letter must include:

  • A formal salutation (Estimada coordinadora:)
  • An introductory sentence stating the purpose
  • At least 3 uses of gustar (including formal equivalents from Step 1)
  • At least 3 uses of ser + adjective to describe the hobby
  • A closing with a formal request (Le solicitamos que…)
